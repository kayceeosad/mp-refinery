Oil & Gas Exploration & Drilling in Kazakhstan

Discovering Energy Resources Through Advanced Exploration Technologies

Munay Pererabotka Refinery is committed to advancing Kazakhstan's energy future through world-class oil and gas exploration, geological evaluation, and drilling operations. By combining advanced seismic technologies, geological research, reservoir analysis, and innovative exploration techniques, we identify and develop high-potential hydrocarbon resources that support sustainable production and long-term energy security. Our exploration programmes are designed to maximise resource recovery while maintaining the highest standards of operational safety, environmental responsibility, and regulatory compliance.

Integrated Exploration & Field Development Expertise

Our multidisciplinary exploration teams integrate geological studies, geophysical surveys, reservoir modelling, and drilling expertise to evaluate and develop new hydrocarbon reserves efficiently. Through advanced project planning and modern exploration technologies, we minimise operational risks, optimise development timelines, and improve production efficiency. Kazakhstan's strategic location and established energy infrastructure further strengthen our ability to deliver reliable petroleum resources to regional and international markets.

Advanced Exploration Technologies

Innovation is at the heart of our exploration strategy. We utilise state-of-the-art seismic imaging, geological modelling, reservoir simulation, satellite mapping, artificial intelligence, and advanced data analytics to identify promising oil and gas reserves with greater accuracy. These technologies enable informed decision-making, reduce exploration uncertainty, improve drilling success rates, and support environmentally responsible resource development.

Our Exploration Capabilities

Our exploration capabilities cover every stage of the upstream energy value chain, including geological and geophysical surveys, 2D and 3D seismic data acquisition, reservoir evaluation, exploration drilling, appraisal programmes, hydrocarbon resource assessment, well planning, field development, environmental impact assessments, and integrated upstream oil and gas solutions. By combining technical expertise with cutting-edge technologies, we maximise exploration success while ensuring safe, efficient, and sustainable operations.

Global Exploration & Sustainable Resource Development

Munay Pererabotka Refinery continues to expand its exploration portfolio through strategic investments, responsible resource development, and international partnerships. Our exploration and appraisal programmes focus on discovering new hydrocarbon reserves, increasing production capacity, and supporting long-term energy security for Kazakhstan and global markets. By combining innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable business practices, we remain committed to delivering reliable energy solutions while creating lasting value for our customers, partners, and stakeholders.

Partner With Our Exploration Experts

Whether you require exploration support, field development expertise, or integrated upstream oil and gas solutions, Munay Pererabotka Refinery combines technical excellence, advanced technologies, and decades of industry experience to deliver sustainable results throughout Kazakhstan and international energy markets. Our commitment to innovation, safety, and operational excellence makes us a trusted partner in the global energy industry.

Munay Pererabotka Refinery sells oil, gas and oil products in the domestic and international markets, optimally distributing flows depending on the market conditions.
