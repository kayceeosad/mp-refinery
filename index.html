Munay Pererabotka Refinery is the undisputed leader of its industry in terms of processing efficiency. Its processing depth is above 90%, while more than 71% of light petroleum products are produced in Euro-5 ecological class. Natural gas is sold to consumers in the Republic of Kazakhstan and on international market as LNG, aviation kerosene JP54 / A1, diesel D2 / D6, mazut M100 and other crude oil products. The company sells natural gas to customers in the Kazakhstan Republic and on the global market as Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), Jet Fuel JP54/A1, Diesel D2/D6, M100 Mazut and other petroleum commodities.
