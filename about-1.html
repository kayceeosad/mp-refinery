Welcome to Munay Pererabotka Refinery

Kazakhstan's Trusted Oil & Gas and Energy Partner

  • Oil Exploration & Drilling
  • Oil & Gas Refining & Processing
  • Fuel Sales, Marketing & Distribution

Munay Pererabotka Refinery is one of Kazakhstan's leading integrated oil and gas companies, providing world-class solutions in oil & gas production, petroleum exploration, crude oil refining, wholesale petroleum trading, and international energy commerce. Headquartered in the Republic of Kazakhstan, we serve industrial, commercial, and government clients with premium petroleum products and reliable energy solutions across domestic and international markets.

As a trusted supplier in the global energy market, Munay Pererabotka Refinery produces and distributes a comprehensive range of petroleum products, including Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), Jet Fuel JP54/A1, Diesel D2/D6, M100 Mazut, aviation fuels, and other refined petroleum products. Our extensive wholesale trading and international trade network enables us to deliver reliable energy solutions to customers across the Republic of Kazakhstan and international markets.

Our business strategy is centred on sustainable growth, operational efficiency, and continuous innovation throughout the energy value chain. We are committed to expanding our oil production, petroleum exploration, refining capacity, and international trading operations while investing in advanced technologies that enhance productivity, reduce operational costs, and improve environmental performance. Through ongoing modernisation of our refining facilities and optimisation of business processes, we continue to strengthen our position as a trusted global energy partner.

As one of the largest and most respected energy companies in the Republic of Kazakhstan, Munay Pererabotka Refinery operates as a vertically integrated enterprise with a diversified portfolio spanning upstream, midstream, and downstream oil & gas operations. Our industrial network includes oil and gas production facilities, petroleum refineries, petrochemical manufacturing plants, fuel storage and distribution terminals, a nationwide network of filling stations, and specialised energy services. In addition, the company maintains strategic investments in the financial sector, including banking and insurance, further supporting long-term business stability and sustainable development.

Driven by innovation, operational excellence, and responsible resource management, Munay Pererabotka Refinery continues to power industries, strengthen global energy supply chains, and deliver high-quality petroleum products through reliable refining, wholesale trading, and international energy solutions. Our dedication to safety, environmental stewardship, and customer satisfaction positions us as a preferred partner for governments, businesses, and industrial clients worldwide. .

Our Strategic Goals:

  • We continuously enhance oil and gas production by maintaining existing assets, expanding exploration activities, and responsibly developing new hydrocarbon resources. Through advanced technologies and efficient field management, we maximise production capacity while ensuring long-term sustainability.
  • We invest in modern refining technologies, digital transformation, and engineering innovation to improve operational efficiency, optimise costs, and deliver high-quality petroleum products. Our commitment to continuous improvement enables us to remain competitive in the evolving global energy market.
  • Safety, environmental stewardship, and regulatory compliance are at the heart of our operations. We maintain world-class standards in operational safety, environmental protection, and sustainable energy practices, ensuring responsible resource management for future generations.
  • We continually optimise our production processes, refining operations, and international trading activities to improve efficiency, expand market reach, and create lasting value. Through strategic partnerships and responsible business practices, we aim to remain a trusted energy partner in Kazakhstan and across global markets.

    Integrated Energy Solutions

    Munay Pererabotka Refinery delivers integrated energy solutions across the entire oil and gas value chain, supporting industries and economies in Kazakhstan and international markets. Our expertise spans oil & gas production, petroleum exploration, crude oil refining, wholesale trading, and international energy trade, enabling us to provide reliable, high-quality petroleum products that meet the evolving demands of customers worldwide.

    Through advanced refining technologies and a commitment to operational excellence, we produce a diverse portfolio of premium petroleum products, including Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), Jet Fuel JP54/A1, Diesel D2/D6, M100 Mazut, gasoline, industrial fuels, and other refined petroleum products. Every product is manufactured to meet stringent international quality and environmental standards, ensuring safety, reliability, and consistent performance.

    Our integrated supply chain, strategic partnerships, and efficient logistics network allow us to deliver energy solutions to commercial, industrial, and government clients with reliability and precision. We continuously invest in innovation, infrastructure, and modern processing technologies to improve refining efficiency, expand production capacity, and strengthen our position as a trusted energy partner.
